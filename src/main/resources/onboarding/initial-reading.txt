On Saturday morning, Maya walked to the small market near the river. She wanted to buy fresh bread and fruit for a picnic with her cousin. The sky was cloudy, but the air felt warm. At the market, an old shopkeeper smiled and offered her a bright red apple. Maya thanked him and continued toward the bridge. When the rain began, she found shelter under a tree and shared her bread with a hungry dog. Soon the clouds moved away, and Maya reached the park just in time for lunch.
